Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

MATS Nepal (Maitri Apartment and Travel Services Pvt. Ltd.) is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you make a booking, enquiry, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, travel dates, and payment details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your travel bookings
  • To communicate with you about your trips and enquiries
  • To send relevant travel offers and updates (with your consent)
  • To improve our services and website experience
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted service partners (hotels, airlines, transport providers) solely to fulfil your booking. All partners are required to handle your data with the same care we do.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience and analyse traffic. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function as expected.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or misuse. Payment information is handled securely and is never stored on our servers.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To make a request, contact us at info@mats.com.np.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their policies separately.
